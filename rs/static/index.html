Options

Funding tokens

Per chain, the tokens a pool may swap into its own when it holds them (USDC added to it, say; largest balance first). Each chain starts with its USDC and USDT; an empty list turns funding off on that chain.

Parking whitelist

While a strategy with parking on waits out of its pool in one token, that token earns in the pegged pool listed for it here, until the strategy needs it again. Parked tokens are kept apart from that pool's own positions. Turn parking on under Options, then on each strategy (Strategy lab).

Chains & RPC

Where the app reads chain data and sends transactions. Defaults are dRPC's free public endpoints (3 requests per batch, about 40 requests a minute, reads grouped with Multicall3). Use your own endpoint for more speed or privacy; it is checked for the right chain before saving. Gas sets how each chain's transactions are priced: Normal is the node's suggested priority fee, Low and Instant what the last 20 blocks paid at the 10th and 90th percentile; it applies to the next transaction.
